# Unslop pass

Done with the unslop skill (by Damir Sokolovsky, @heydamir, CC BY 4.0), 24 November 2026.

## Audit

Slop level: light. A restrained editorial portfolio already (warm paper, Instrument Serif, Inter Tight, square corners, the photographs carrying all the color), so this was polish mode.

- Uppercase tracked labels nearly everywhere: nav, menu button, section labels, "All collections" links, the hero line under the name, buttons, form labels, collection facts, the back link, client list title, the "Most booked" tag
- Italic for emphasis in headlines: "Services *and* pricing", "Hello, I'm *Maren*.", "Out of *frame.*", the footer's "*Let's talk.*", and titles turning italic on hover
- A greeting headline on About ("Hello, I'm Maren.")
- Trailing periods on headlines and short lines: the home statement, the hero line ("Photographer. Based in Halden Bay, working everywhere."), "Let's talk.", "Out of frame."
- Zero-padded collection numbers ("01", "02")
- Photographs zooming on hover (collection cards and gallery tiles)
- Labels and prices at weight 600 with wide tracking
- A soft text shadow under the hero name

## Direction

- Product: the portfolio of a landscape and portrait photographer, to show collections and take commissions
- Character: patient, spare, salt-aired
- World: a northern shore at first light, contact sheets, a farmhouse window
- Moments: the full-bleed hero photograph and each collection's justified gallery
- Signature: the contact-sheet caption under each collection ("6 photographs, 2026") and the year and count on its page

## What changed

- Spacing: labels sit 16px above their headings everywhere
- Color: unchanged palette (paper, ink, one hairline); the hero's text shadow is gone, the scrim stays for legibility
- Copy: About opens with the name instead of a greeting; no periods on headlines or the hero line ("Photographer, based in Halden Bay, working everywhere"); the Services page label says Commissions so it doesn't repeat the title
- Type: every label, nav link, button and form label in sentence case at normal tracking, weight 400 to 500; no italic anywhere (the italic font file is no longer loaded); collection numbers plain 1 to 4
- Details: buttons keep the square editorial corners, in sentence case; hovering a title underlines it instead of switching to italic
- Motion: photographs no longer zoom on hover, they dim slightly; one easing curve for every transition
